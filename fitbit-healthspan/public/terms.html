Not a medical device

Vitalspan is a general wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition. Recovery, Strain, Sleep, Stress, Health Monitor and Body Age are estimates calculated from consumer wearable data and published research. They can be inaccurate. Do not use them to make medical decisions. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your health, and seek emergency care when needed.

Body Age

Body Age is an "effective age" estimate based on population studies of lifestyle factors and all-cause mortality. It is not a clinical biological-age test, and it does not predict your lifespan.

Exercise
