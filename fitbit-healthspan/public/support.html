Connecting your Fitbit

  1. Install the Google Health app (formerly Fitbit), sign in with Google, and finish moving your Fitbit account if asked.
  2. In Vitalspan, tap Connect and approve read-only access.
  3. The first sync downloads about 200 days and can take a minute or two.

Connecting Apple Health (Apple Watch, Oura, Garmin and more)

  1. In your device's own app (for example Oura, Garmin Connect or Withings), turn on sharing with Apple Health.
  2. In Vitalspan, open More → Devices & sources and tap Connect next to Apple Health, then allow the data types you want to share.
  3. Each app or device found in Apple Health appears as its own device. Connect Fitbit as well if you wear one; Vitalspan merges them.

Using several devices

Vitalspan picks the device with the best recent data for each metric (rings and bands are preferred overnight) and never mixes two devices' HRV or resting heart rate from night to night, so your personal baselines stay consistent. You can choose the device for each metric, or switch a device off, under More → Devices & sources.

Common questions

"Account not linked" error. Your Fitbit account hasn't moved to Google yet. Open the Google Health app and complete the migration.

Why is Recovery grey? The score is low-confidence, for example because sleep wasn't staged or there was too little heart-rate data. Wear your tracker snugly overnight.

Why is Body Age locked? It needs 21 nights of data in 31 days, plus your biological sex (Google doesn't share it), which you can set in Settings.

Why isn't VO₂max in my Body Age? Trackers without GPS estimate VO₂max from resting heart rate and age, which Body Age already counts. GPS-tracked runs produce a VO₂max that counts.

Apple Health shows no data. Check the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Vitalspan and turn on the data types. iPhone doesn't tell apps which types were declined, so Vitalspan can only see what you've allowed.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Delete all data. This also revokes Google access. Data in Apple Health itself is never changed.
