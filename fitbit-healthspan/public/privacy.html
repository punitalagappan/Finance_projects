Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Summary: Vitalspan has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your health data goes directly from Google to your device, is processed only on your device, and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Who we are

Vitalspan ("the app", "we") is an independent wellness app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google, Fitbit or any wearable manufacturer. Contact: support@example.com.

Data the app reads

  • From your Google account, only if you connect it, with read-only permission you grant on Google's sign-in screen: activity and fitness (steps, exercise sessions, VO₂max), health metrics (heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate, SpO₂, skin temperature, weight and body fat), sleep, your profile age, and optionally your paired device model.
  • From files you choose to import (for example a Google Takeout / Fitbit export).
  • What you type in: age, biological sex, tracker model, wake time, optional body fat %, and journal answers.

Where your data goes

  • All processing happens on your device. Your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. Google sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain and are not synced to iCloud Keychain.
  • The only network requests the app makes are to Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com and health.googleapis.com) to sign you in and download your own data. We operate no server that receives your data.
  • We do not sell, share, rent or use your data for advertising, marketing, data brokerage or AI model training. We do not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Vitalspan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show you your own health and fitness scores inside the app. No human reads it, and it is not transferred to anyone.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device (bedtime and morning). No push server is involved.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Settings → Delete all data erases all health data, journal entries and settings from the device and revokes the app's Google access. Deleting the app also removes its data. You can also revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Vitalspan is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date, and material changes will be shown in the app.
