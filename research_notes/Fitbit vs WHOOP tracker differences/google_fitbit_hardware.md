## 2. Per-device sensors and hardware

### Takeaway
All current Google wearables use wrist-worn optical PPG plus red/IR SpO2 and an accelerometer; Pixel Watch 2-5 add multipath PPG, cEDA, ECG and a dedicated skin-temperature sensor (far-field on PW4/PW5). Fitbit Air has no screen, no GPS and no ECG app; Fitbit Edge adds ECG, GPS (+Galileo), altimeter, a 1.34" screen and a user-replaceable battery, but drops the Charge 6's on-demand EDA scan. LED counts/wavelengths beyond "green PPG + red/IR SpO2" were not found.

## 3. Which metrics each device supports in the Google Health app

Core nightly metrics (HRV, RHR, breathing rate, SpO2, skin-temp variation, sleep stages, Sleep Score) and Daily Readiness are broadly available across the 2021+ lineup; Cardio Load/Target Load extended from Pixel Watch 3 to all readiness-capable devices (and, from app 5.09, to third-party workouts). Device-specific: ECG (Charge 6, Sense 2, Edge, Pixel Watch), Loss of Pulse (Pixel Watch 3+, region-limited), Blood Pressure Trends and Insulin Resistance Trends (Pixel Watch 5, data collection from Sept 2026, first reports Oct 2026). Fitbit Air has FDA-cleared background AFib detection but no ECG app; early reviews reported Readiness/Cardio Load/AZM not populating at launch.

## 4. How Fitbit measures HRV, RHR, breathing rate, skin temperature, and intraday HR sampling

### Takeaway
HRV is RMSSD computed from optical beat intervals during the main (longest, >3 h) sleep, reported as a nightly value aggregated from 5-minute windows; whether deep sleep specifically is used is inconsistently documented. Intraday HR is commonly reported as 1-second resolution during tracked exercise and ~5-second (variably 5-15 s) at other times; these figures come from secondary sources, not current official docs.

### Cited Findings
- Fitbit uses RMSSD to determine HRV from heart-rate data; optical sensor estimates time between beats — [Google Health Help: heart rate](https://support.google.com/fitbit/answer/14237938?hl=en); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2300292/what-fitbit-heart-rate-variability-tells-you/)
- HRV uses the longest sleep period in the previous 24 h; only periods >3 h qualify (secondary source) — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2300292/what-fitbit-heart-rate-variability-tells-you/)
- Research study: Fitbit computes RMSSD over 5-min intervals overnight; user sees an aggregated (median per study summary) nocturnal value — [ResearchGate: Versa 4 nocturnal HRV agreement study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/397730635_Agreement_between_Fitbit_Versa_4_and_a_reference_standard_on_time-domain_nocturnal_heart_rate_variability_A_pilot_study)
- Charge 4 study: moderate-to-strong agreement with reference HRV, tendency to underestimate — per search summary of [PMC12300306](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12300306/)
- Sampling: HR stored at 1-s intervals during exercise tracking and 5-s intervals otherwise (older Charge HR/Surge) — [DC Rainmaker 2016](https://www.dcrainmaker.com/2016/02/understanding-continual-optical.html); Charge 6 reportedly records every second during exercise and every 5 s otherwise — [Welltory Charge 6](https://welltory.com/blog/fitbit-charge-6-features)
- Conflicting: Fitbit Community says exercise mode typically 1-3 s, otherwise 5-15 s — [Fitbit Community](https://community.fitbit.com/t5/SDK-Development/Heart-Rate-Sensor-Frequency/td-p/4053856?nobounce); Charge 4 study observed 1-5 s in training mode — [PMC8924780](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8924780/)
- All of Us research program publishes considerations for Fitbit data (sampling/aggregation caveats) — [All of Us](https://support.researchallofus.org/hc/en-us/articles/9651723386388-Considerations-while-using-Fitbit-Data-in-the-All-of-Us-Research-Program)
- Air skin temp: deviation from personal nightly baseline; SpO2 sampled periodically by day and continuously overnight (third-party explainer) — [ennap.com](https://ennap.com/blogs/articles/google-fitbit-air-specs)

### Inferences
- Since Pixel Watch 2, HR is produced by on-device deep learning over multipath PPG ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.21436)); Charge 6 / Air / Edge reportedly reuse Pixel-derived algorithms, so beat-interval quality likely differs between Pixel Watch (multipath) and Fitbit bands.
- Apps should expect intraday HR at irregular ~1-15 s cadence and should not assume uniform sampling.

### Gaps
- Official current help text could not be fetched. **[background knowledge, not verified this session]**: (a) Fitbit Web API HRV endpoint returns `dailyRmssd` (main sleep) and `deepRmssd` (deep sleep), with intraday HRV in 5-min windows — the deep-sleep linkage likely originates here; (b) breathing rate is computed during deep sleep, falling back to light sleep if deep sleep is insufficient; (c) RHR is computed daily from both awake (still) and sleep HR data; (d) skin-temperature variation is shown relative to a personal baseline established after about 3 nights (task brief's "30-day baseline" not confirmed — Fitbit has used ~30-day personal ranges for the Health Metrics dashboard, so both may coexist).
- No 2026 Google doc on whether Air/Edge change sampling cadence.
